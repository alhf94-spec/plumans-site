Keep good tech in use.

Millions of working phones, tablets and watches sit unused in drawers while new ones are made to replace them. Plumans gives them a second life: we find them, check them properly and pass them on with an honest description, at a fair price.

Our mission

To make second-hand the obvious first choice, by making it as trustworthy as buying new.

A device used for longer is a new one that doesn't need to be made. What holds people back from buying used is doubt: is it what the photos show, is the battery tired, is it locked to someone else? We remove that doubt, one checked device at a time.

What we stand for

Reuse first

We only sell devices that already exist, and keep them working and out of landfill for longer.

Radical honesty

Every mark, crack and battery figure goes in the description. If we found it, you'll read about it.

AI with a human hand

AI agents built on Claude do the checking and record-keeping. A person approves every purchase and every sale.

How we work

  1. Checked on arrival. IMEI or serial number verified, battery health recorded, account locks confirmed off.
  2. Every defect disclosed. The listing says exactly what we found, with photos of the real unit.
  3. One unit, one stock code. The device you receive is exactly the one that was photographed and tested.
