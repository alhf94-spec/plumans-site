Quality second-hand electronics, tested and described honestly.

Plumans is a small UK business that sources used phones, tablets, smartwatches and consoles, checks every one, and resells it with an accurate description. We started in September 2026.

See our current stock on eBay UK

How we work

  1. Every device is checked on arrival. IMEI or serial number verified, battery health recorded, and account locks confirmed off.
  2. Every defect is listed. If it has a mark, a crack or a weak battery, the listing says so.
  3. Each unit gets its own stock code, so the item you buy is exactly the one that was photographed and tested.

Built with AI

We run our operations with a team of AI agents, built on Claude, that handle sourcing alerts, stock records and listing drafts — with a person approving every purchase and sale.
